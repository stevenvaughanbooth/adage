How it works

Your agents already wrote the manual. Adage reads it.

  1. 01

    Read the traces

    Claude Code leaves a transcript of every session, and Adage reads it where it already is, on your machine. There is nothing to instrument. Codex and OpenCode adapters are in preview.

  2. 02

    Condition reflexes

    adage condition keeps the steps that got work done and compiles them into reflexes: small, typed rules with their evidence attached. Dead ends become inhibit reflexes.

  3. 03

    Deliver at the hooks

    When the same moment comes back, a hook matches it and hands the agent a one-line directive. The model still decides; it just doesn't have to rediscover the repository.

Built to stay out of the way

It speaks up only when it matches. Otherwise it isn't there.

  • Never executes

    The adage binary never runs your repository's commands. Your agent runs every step through its own tools, under its harness's permissions. Delegating a step is opt-in, per reflex, inside an OS sandbox.

  • Fails open

    If Adage errors or runs out of time at a hook, the agent carries on as if it were not installed.

  • Evidence attached

    Every reflex carries its distinct sessions and a Wilson lower bound. Review any of them, then strengthen, weaken or extinguish it.

  • Yours, on disk

    Traces stay on your machine. Reflexes live in tracks on disk and travel as signed packs you can review, commit and share.

Get started

One binary. Three commands.

adage init finds your harnesses, installs the hooks (showing you the diff first) and conditions reflexes from the traces you already have. The first release is on its way; early access gets it first.
