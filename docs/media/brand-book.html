Put your agent
on rails.

Adage gives coding agents instincts and reflexes. Instincts come in the box. Reflexes are conditioned from what your agents actually did in your repo. Both fire in five milliseconds on the CPU, so the model only reasons about what is new.

This book is the source of truth for how Adage is named, described, drawn and animated. The film, the README, the CLI and the website should all be recognizably the same object after reading it.

01 · The idea

Reasoning is paid every session. A reflex is paid once.

Every coding agent starts every session from zero. It re-discovers the lookup command, re-reads the same files, re-runs the test command that never worked. Each of those decisions is an inference call on a GPU, paid for again and again.

Adage records what agents did, learns the moves that repeat, and replays them as text signals through the harness's hooks. The known route runs deterministically on the CPU; the model keeps the novel work. The result is fewer turns, a lower bill, and fewer missed steps, measured on real recorded sessions rather than claimed.

One sentence: Adage puts coding agents on rails. It learns the routes your agents repeat in a repository and replays them as text signals in 5 ms on the CPU, so the model only reasons about what is new.

Not memory. Behaviour, with evidence. Rules files are unconditional and rot; a reflex fires at the moment, carries its evidence, and retires when the evidence fades.

What it is

A 10 MB Rust binary that wires into your agent harness's hooks (Claude Code today; OpenCode and Codex adapters in progress), records sessions, learns routes, and injects them as signals. Apache-2.0.

What it is not

Not a model. Not a wrapper. Not an agent. It never runs what it learned, never calls an API at decision time, and fails open. If Adage is wrong, the agent just proceeds as it would have.

Who it is for

Developers who run coding agents daily and can feel the waste. Engineering leads who pay the inference bill. Platform teams who want their repo's tribal knowledge to survive the next session.

02 · The primitives

Two nouns carry the whole product.

Everything Adage knows is either an instinct or a reflex. An instinct is given; a reflex is conditioned by repetition. In the index the line is the provenance field: shipped or authored routes on one side, routes with desire-path provenance and evidence on the other. Use the words exactly this way, everywhere, and never capitalise either as a product name.

Instincts

born with it · ships in the box

Routes Adage comes with, or tracks you install. They work on the first session with zero history: the moves that are true of nearly every repo, and the tracks a team or a community has already learned and shared.

  • Fire from day one. No recording required.
  • Arrive amber. Your own sessions confirm them green. That is a trust story, not a limitation.
  • A reflex someone else learned becomes your instinct when you adopt it, evidence attached and marked as theirs.

Reflexes

learned · from your recorded sessions

Routes Adage conditioned from what agents did in this repository, with evidence attached: how many sessions took the move, how often it held, how many turns it saved. A dead end is a reflex too: flinching away from the command that never works here. Nobody writes the first draft.

  • Learned by adage learn, refreshed by auto-learn after every session.
  • Carry evidence: 23 of 33 sessions, lower bound 0.53, 8 turns saved.
  • Change aspect as evidence changes. Never written by hand.

Aspects are the lamps

Green do this. Amber consider this. Red dead end: avoid this, do that instead. The lamp encodes the statistical lower bound on the evidence, not the raw rate: green needs enough sessions to be sure. The schema calls amber "yellow"; marketing says amber.

Signals are what the agent sees

An instinct or reflex that fires becomes a short text block in the agent's context, with its trigger, its step and its provenance. Text only. The agent decides; Adage never executes.

Tracks are how they travel

Instincts and reflexes are grouped into versioned tracks (acme-billing/work-items@0.1.0). Tracks are what you share, install, pin and diff.

03 · The story

Want, obstacle, turn, proof, ask.

Want

Agents that get faster at your repo the more they work in it, the way a new engineer does.

Obstacle

They forget everything between sessions. Same maze, every time, at GPU prices. Rules files help, but someone has to write them and nobody keeps them true.

Turn

Adage records the wandering, learns the route, and replays it as a reflex the next time the pattern appears. Nobody writes the first draft. The GPU explored once; the route is on disk.

Proof

On recorded sessions: first edit at turn 2 instead of 20, 61% less per task, hidden checks passed 100% instead of 78%. Same task, same model, same day.

Ask

brew install stevenvaughanbooth/tap/adage && adage init. Two commands, under a minute, fail-open. Instincts on day one; the first reflex after a handful of sessions.

Messaging pillars

Instincts on day one.

Useful in the first session, before anything has been recorded. Shipped packs, shared tracks.

proof · a track installs with one command · packs are versioned and signed

Reflexes by Friday.

Every session teaches it. Reflexes appear with evidence and change as the evidence changes. Nobody writes the first draft.

proof · 54 sessions learned in 0.24 s · 4 reflexes with support and rate on screen

The known route skips the GPU.

A reflex fires in 5 ms on the CPU with no inference. The model reads a hint instead of rediscovering it, and keeps the novel work. The bill falls as the reflexes accumulate.

04 · Voice

Plain, physical, measured.

Plain

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. A developer should be able to repeat any line to a coworker without translating it.

Physical

The world is a railway and the nouns run on it: rail, track, signal, lamp, dead end, finish line, and the instincts and reflexes that travel them. Not brains, not magic, not "AI-powered".

Measured

Every number has a denominator and a source. "Recorded, not staged." If a claim cannot be backed by a session file, it does not go on screen.

Say

  • "Adage learned a reflex." A concrete event with evidence.
  • "Known routes skip the GPU." Precise about what moves off the model. Never "skips the brain": a signal goes into the model's context.
  • "Recorded, not staged." The credibility line. Use it whenever real footage is on screen.
  • "Fail-open." The safety property, in the industry's own word.
  • "Text only. Never runs what it learned." The trust property, scoped honestly: auto-learn runs Adage's own learner, never a learned command.

Never say

  • "AI-powered", "intelligent", "smart". Adage's whole point is that decision time has no model in it.
  • "Replaces the GPU" or "no more inference". The model still does the novel work. Say "skips the GPU for the known route".
  • "Instinct" or "Reflex" capitalized as a product name. They are feature nouns. See guardrails.
  • "Memory", "remembers", "forgets". Everyone claims memory. Adage conditions behaviour, with evidence, and never asks the model what it remembers.
  • "Automates", "autopilot". Adage advises; the agent acts.

06 · Identity

A wordmark, three lamps, one rail.

The identity is deliberately spare so the product footage can carry the colour. The wordmark is Instrument Serif with a vertical white-to-violet gradient. The three lamps are the aspects, always in this order, always lit. The rail is a single glowing line: the route, paved. Together they are the only three marks Adage owns. Known risk: near-black, violet and a serif with one italic word is the 2026 default for AI companies. What is ours is the orange-to-violet story (heat cooling into rail) and the lamps; lean on those, not on the palette.

Wordmark rules

Always "Adage", never all-caps in prose; the film's giant set is the one exception. Minimum clear space equals the height of the "A". Gradient on dark only; flat ink on light. Never outline, never drop shadow.

Lamp rules

Green, amber, red, left to right, equal size, 1.4 diameters apart, and at least 1.8 diameters from any window chrome so they never read as macOS controls. On dark they glow; on light they are flat and darkened for contrast. Never fewer than three. Never used as bullet points.

Primitive glyphs

Instinct: a filled dot inside a ring (born with it). Reflex: a dot at the peak of an arc landing on a green dot (the learned path). Line weight 2.4 at 56 px. Use them in the UI next to the route id, never as logos.

Serif for the claim

Headlines and big numbers only. Italic marks the word that carries the turn ("steps in", "Less GPU"). One italic per line, never two.

Sans for the explanation

Weight 300 for captions and body, 400 for labels under numbers, 500 for UI. Minimum 28 px under any number on film, nothing under 20 px anywhere on film; 16 px in product.

Mono for anything real

Commands, route ids, hook lines, evidence, timestamps, and the closing chips. If it is mono, it came from the product, the data, or it is a fact chip. Never set a headline or a caption in mono.

09 · Motion

Light travels; text arrives.

The rail reveals

Routes are drawn by draw-range along their curve, never faded in. A route being learned glows before it is paved. A firing reflex sends a white-hot pulse down the segment ahead.

Text rises through a mask

Every headline enters from below through a clipped mask with a quartic ease-out over 0.8 s and leaves by rising 18 px while fading. No slides, no scale-pops on text.

Camera is a dolly, not a drone

Long, steady moves with centripetal easing between keys. Pinned UI never moves with the camera. One push-in per turn of the story, never on a caption.

Bloom is the light source

Unreal bloom at strength 1.25, radius 0.55, threshold 0.28. Only rails, lamps, the comet, the die and the chip are allowed above threshold. Product panels render after bloom so text stays crisp.

Sound follows the picture

112 BPM. Hits on reveals, ticks on hook events, a riser before every set piece. Silence under the wordmark.

Reduced motion

On the web, honour prefers-reduced-motion: lamps stop breathing, rails appear drawn, text fades.

13 · Guardrails

What keeps this defensible.

The words are descriptive, not brands

"instinct" and "reflex" are lowercase, plural, possessed feature nouns describing what a route is: your agent's instincts, its reflexes. Never "Adage Instinct", "Adage Reflex", "Instinct by Adage", a tier named either, or "reflex layer" as a category label. AMD holds INSTINCT marks for GPUs (and the film shows a GPU), a $2.5B AI-assistant company trades as Instinct, and Pynecone holds a REFLEX application in class 42 for an AI app platform. Descriptive use inside a product called Adage is ordinary; anything that starts to identify the source invites a letter.

The name is Adage

Adage is the only mark to file, and it is a common word, so clear it first. Class 9 (downloadable software) and class 42 (SaaS, software development tools). adage.dev and adage.sh are registered by someone; adage.ai, adage.run and adage.io looked free at last check. Register before the film ships publicly.

Claims are measured

Always name the model, the task count and the repo next to a number. "Measured on recorded sessions" is stronger than any adjective and cannot be argued with.

Real footage stays real

Product panels in the film are captures of the binary or faithful renderings of its output for the recorded session. Where the film or this book shows a screen ahead of the product (instinct badges, listing aliases), it is a target for the next release and the product catches up before the film goes public. Keep the content true to the recorded data even when the chrome is ahead of the binary.
