# v5 · typography and motion-type review

## Verdict

Right family (Instrument Serif display, Plex Sans captions, Plex Mono evidence), right idea (words rise out of blur). Wrong discipline. `index5.html` uses **eleven serif sizes, eight sans, nine mono** (28 distinct) where a launch film uses four. One reveal recipe is applied to everything: the stagger is normalised to line length (`.55/n`), so a 4-word headline lands at 137 ms/word and a 14-word caption at 39 ms/word and smears (t025, t021, t059). Blur is a fixed 14 px, which is 0.08 em on a headline and 0.4 em on a caption. Exits are linear, simultaneous and 450 ms, a reversed entry rather than a release. Twelve text styles sit below a 22 px floor at 1080p. Counters jump by `textContent`. And at the two moments type must own the frame, the numerals (64–73) and the wordmark (80–91), rails and nodes run straight through the glyphs. Fixable in a day; nothing needs new assets.

## The type system to adopt

- **Display** 144 px Instrument Serif 400, `letter-spacing -.02em`, `line-height 1.02`, `#f4f4f7`. Italic for the one operative word. Max 6 words a line, `max-width 1400px`. Left variant 96 px at left 120 when a panel is on the right.
- **Title** 96 px serif (session headlines, close tagline).
- **Body** 40 px IBM Plex Sans 400, `#d5d7e2`, `letter-spacing 0`, whole-line reveal.
- **Detail** 24 px Plex Mono 400 `#b4b7c4` (tags, chips, cards); 18 px only for tracked-caps labels (`.12em`, `#7a7d8c`). Nothing smaller exists.
- **Numerals** 360 px serif solid white (64–73); counters and stats 120 px, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, label 28 px sans; wordmark 288 px.
- **Motion**: enter `eo` (CSS `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`), 80 ms/word, 650 ms/word, `.35em` rise, `.1em` blur. Exit `ei3` (`cubic-bezier(.32,0,.67,0)`), 300 ms, 25 ms reverse stagger, `−.25em`, `.06em` blur. Cards: 350 ms, 10 px rise, no blur. Beat cross-fade stays 120 ms.
- **Contrast rule**: no Display or Numeral over a rail or a node field brighter than 15 %; dim the world or scrim (`rgba(5,5,7,.55)` radial, 60 % stop) before adding a shadow.

## Three signature moves

1. **Odometer digits.** Every counter renders each digit as a 1 em clipped column; on a tick the old digit slides to `−1em`, the new rises from `+1em`, 180 ms `eo`, 3 px blur at mid-travel. Deterministic: `frac = ((t−t0) % step)/step` with `step` = .3 / .1 / .43 / .45 for wander / maze / ghost / fleet. The wander count "24 thoughts" then reads as a meter running, not a caption changing.
2. **The reflex leaves last.** In `kinetic()`, the `<i>` word holds 200 ms after the others exit and is the last to land on entry (word stagger runs from the plain words to the italic). "reflex" is in six headlines; by the close the eye waits for it. Cost: one flag in `show()`.
3. **Numerals rise out of the letterbox.** The 70 px bars appear at 64 s and now have a job: wrap `.big` in `top:70px; bottom:70px; overflow:hidden`, enter from `translateY(100%)` (700 ms `eo`), exit to `translateY(−100%)` (280 ms `ei3`); the evidence line lives inside the top bar. "−61%", "10×", "0" arrive like a train from the bottom bar and depart into the top one, with black behind the eyebrow guaranteed.
